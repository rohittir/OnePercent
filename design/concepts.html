OnePercent: three directions

Each direction shows the same three moments: the home screen, playing a game, and finishing the day. The four games and the 1%-a-day rule stay the same; only the world around them changes.

A · Percy and pals

A friendly sprout buddy, Percy, grows a little every day you play. Bright, chunky, tactile buttons. The warmest fit for 7–10, and still fine for older kids.

B · Brain galaxy

Each skill is a planet and each day is a mission; your rocket flies 1% farther every day. Darker and cooler, so it lands better with 11–14 without losing younger kids.

C · Grow garden

Your tree grows one ring a day, and each skill is a fruit you ripen by playing. Calm, warm and soft; it suits mindful parents and younger kids best, and is the least "game-y".
